About BISO

BISO is an organisation built on one idea: space exploration works best when countries, researchers and young people work together.

Mission

To bring people together across India and the world to explore space and share the results openly.

Vision

A future where space science is open to every curious mind, and India is a leading voice in it.

Values

Curiosity, collaboration and openness. We share what we learn and credit everyone who helped.
